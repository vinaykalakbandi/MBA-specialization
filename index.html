Core Principle

No specialization is automatically superior. Fit matters more than fashion.

A good choice connects three things: what you are genuinely interested in, what you are willing to work hard at, and where credible career opportunities exist. The sweet spot is not always obvious, which is why reflection matters more than rankings.
